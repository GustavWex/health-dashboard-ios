Today, two ways

The Today screen redesigned on the thru system, in two card styles. Same grid, type, face and content; only the card material and bar ends differ. Demo data only.

Recovery

A. Glass cards

As the design doc says today

  • Frosted white cards with a hairline and a soft shadow; the glows blur through.
  • Bars and streak squares with rounded ends.
  • Closest to the current app and to iOS 26 Liquid Glass.

B. Flat cards

Recommended: glass only for the tab bar

  • Solid paper-white cards with a 1 pt ink hairline; no blur, no shadow.
  • The face sits straight on the page, like the logo; bars end square, like the letters.
  • Liquid Glass stays on system chrome: the tab bar and sheets.
